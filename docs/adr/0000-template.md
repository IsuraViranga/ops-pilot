## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? Include the constraints that matter:
technical, operational, time, cost, team size. Written so that someone joining the
project in a year understands the pressure without having been there.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly in one or two sentences. Active voice.

> We will ...

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer

### Negative

- What becomes harder, slower or more expensive. Be honest — an ADR with no
  downsides listed is an advertisement, not a decision record.

### Neutral

- What simply changes
